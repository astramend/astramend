About Us

Committed to
a Healthier Tomorrow

AstraMend (OPC) Private Limited is a pharmaceutical healthcare company focused on quality, innovation and excellence, delivering reliable healthcare solutions for a better tomorrow.

AstraMend (OPC) Private Limited collaborates with Santiago Life Sciences, an ISO 9001:2015 certified company with a legacy in pharmaceutical distribution and marketing since 1963. AstraMend also operates through a dedicated division of Arico Life Sciences, strengthening its capabilities in pharmaceutical manufacturing, product development and healthcare distribution.

Through these strategic collaborations and associations, AstraMend aims to provide quality-focused pharmaceutical solutions while building a reliable, sustainable and professionally managed healthcare network.
